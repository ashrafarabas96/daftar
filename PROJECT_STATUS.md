## Open decisions and debt

- **OD-03 — country tax rules: OPEN** (purchase tax, and from Phase 4 sales tax as well). Not a blocker for P3-S1, which was accepted without it. It is a hard dependency, with official-source evidence, before any slice that implements purchase-tax calculation, tax posting, tax-inclusive/exclusive purchases or jurisdiction-specific purchase tax. Until then a non-zero purchase tax is refused (P3-AL-23). The same hard boundary is restated for **sales** tax in P4-S0: no non-zero jurisdiction-specific sales tax may be enabled until an approved Country Pack backed by official legal and tax sources exists; fields may be designed structurally, but the default behaviour works at tax = 0 or refuses a non-zero tax under a stable policy.
- TD-08 — `main` branch protection is not configured and is not verifiable from this tooling (external: repository settings). The six required checks are workspaces, backend, web-admin, android, hygiene and browser. Until it is set externally the compensating policy is mandatory: no direct push to `main`, every change through a pull request, merge only after the required CI, no force push to `main`, no deletion of `main`.
- TD-10 — symmetric assertion signing, bounded to `merchant-api`.
- TD-14 — narrowed in the corrective pass (production `platform-api` refuses `APP_DATABASE_URL`; key install is write-once); the remaining two-credential boundary is exact and targeted at Phase 15.
- TD-15 (no supplier-payment reversal, owned no later than the Phase 4 settlement work), TD-17 (no stored stock rebuild swap, Phase 15), TD-21 (refresh after a lost answer, Phase 15), TD-22 (button tokens and the Turkish companion face, a design-system decision). Closed in the corrective pass: TD-01, TD-06 (Phase 3 web), TD-16, TD-18, TD-19, TD-20. Full register: `TECHNICAL_DEBT.md`.
